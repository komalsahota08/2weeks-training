Online Exam System

Online examinations have emerged as a highly popular and practical solution for global educational institutions. While digital assessments are not entirely new, their integration into formal schooling skyrocketed recently. The inherent flexibility of remote testing allows students from diverse geographical locations to participate without travel constraints, saving both time and resources. Furthermore, computerized evaluation significantly reduces paperwork, helping conserve natural resources and streamline grading systems. Despite these distinct advantages, online formats introduce unique challenges, such as tracking student academic integrity and managing technical connectivity issues. To navigate this landscape successfully, students must develop strong self-discipline, prepare a quiet environment, and strictly respect the boundaries of digital testing platforms
